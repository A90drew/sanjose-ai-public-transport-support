# Transit Translation: AI for Public Transportation Language Barrier solution

Client: City of San José, Valley Transport Authority (VTA)
Team: Andrew Rivas Sumit Chhabra, Brandon Daniel, Santos Sandova, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I helped draft the feasibility analysis, draft the where it breaks, and develop our UI

## The problem
Mai Nguyen, a 70 year old Vietnamese speaking resident. Mai enters a route unable to understand VTA intercom announce the bus won't arrive.

## What we built
- Our prototype allows residents to submit reports regarding any transit information for the VTA. 
- The Prototype supports English, Spanish, Vietnamese and Punjabi.
- These reports are saved and accessible to all users, serving as a space of reliable ride information in different languages
